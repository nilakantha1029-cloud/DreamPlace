About Us

We Turn Dream Trips into Cherished Memories

Dream Place was founded in Hyderabad in 2009 with a simple belief: that every person deserves to experience the world's wonders without the stress of planning. We started as a small boutique travel agency and have grown into one of South India's most trusted travel platforms.

Today, our team of 35 passionate travel experts partners with local ground operators in 28 countries to curate completely personalised journeys for families, honeymooners, solo adventurers and corporate groups.

IATA accredited & Ministry of Tourism certified
24/7 in-trip emergency support worldwide
100% transparent pricing — no hidden charges
Best-price guarantee on all packages
Responsible & eco-conscious travel practices
